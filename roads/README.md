# Roads and street infrastructure

Required modular pieces:
- Straight road segments, lanes and turning lanes
- Curves, T-junctions, four-way intersections and roundabouts
- Sidewalks, kerbs, cycle lanes and accessible curb ramps
- Zebra crossings, lane arrows, stop lines and road markings
- Traffic lights, signs, poles, barriers and street furniture

Keep road markings as reusable decals or geometry. Document real-world dimensions and coordinate conventions. Use generic signage unless rights and local accuracy are verified.
