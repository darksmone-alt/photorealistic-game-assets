# Lighting and atmosphere

Create coherent environment presets rather than isolated lights:
- Clear midday: high sun, crisp but controlled shadows
- Overcast: broad soft illumination and low contrast
- Golden hour: warm low-angle sun and long shadows
- Blue hour: cool ambient sky with emerging practical lights
- Night: dark sky, street lamps, shop windows and vehicle lights

Prefer licensed HDRIs or procedural sky models. Record HDRI source, licence, orientation, exposure and colour-space assumptions. Use physically based exposure and avoid baking lighting into reusable assets unless documented.
