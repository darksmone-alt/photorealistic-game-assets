# Urban Block 01 — procedural 3D starter asset

Original, procedurally generated OBJ geometry for a European city street scene.

## Contents
- 3 building masses: historic mixed-use block, brick residential block with balconies, glass office tower
- Road, sidewalks, kerbs, dashed lane markings and pedestrian crossing
- Street lamps, four street trees and two generic parked cars
- MTL materials with approximate PBR roughness/metalness values

## Technical details
- Format: Wavefront OBJ + MTL
- Units: metres
- Up axis: Y
- Road corridor: 36 m long, 20 m wide including sidewalks
- Front facades face +Z
- Geometry is a blockout/starter kit, not a photogrammetric scan. Replace or augment materials and facade details for final photorealism.
- Import OBJ and keep the accompanying MTL in the same directory.

## Rights
Original procedural geometry created for this repository. No third-party meshes or textures are embedded.
