# Licence and provenance policy

This repository is intended to support a game that may be distributed publicly or commercially. A free download is not automatically free to redistribute.

## Required checks
Before adding any third-party file, verify the licence on the individual asset page and record:
1. Licence name and version (for example CC0 1.0, CC BY 4.0, or the publisher's own terms).
2. Whether commercial use is allowed.
3. Whether modification is allowed.
4. Whether redistribution inside a game or asset bundle is allowed.
5. Attribution wording and placement requirements.
6. Any restrictions on trademarks, likenesses, model releases or separate texture files.

## Repository rules
- Prefer CC0 assets where practical.
- CC BY assets may be used only when attribution can be preserved in the project credits and catalogue.
- Do not include NC (NonCommercial), ND (NoDerivatives), editorial-only, personal-use-only or unclear licences.
- Do not assume a whole platform or collection has one licence; verify each asset.
- Record separately licensed textures, HDRIs, animations and sound files.
- Keep a copy or link to the licence terms and the asset's source page in the catalogue.

## Attribution template
For each CC BY asset, use the creator's requested wording. If none is specified, record:

> “[Asset title]” by [Creator], licensed under [Licence name and version]. Source: [URL]. Changes: [describe changes or “none”].

## Important
This document is an internal intake policy, not legal advice. When terms are ambiguous, do not add the asset until its permitted use is confirmed.
